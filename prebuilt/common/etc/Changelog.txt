ExpressiveOS 1.1.2 for the Pixel — Golden Harvest
==============================

ExpressiveOS 1.1.2 is a small update to 1.1.1: Google's on-device AI apps can reach Private Compute Services again, Circle to Search works, and you can pick Pixel Launcher as your home screen in Expressive Studio.
On 1.1.1, it's a small update made just for your build: open the Updater and install it, and your apps and data stay. On 1.1 or 1.0, the Updater offers the full update, and your apps and data stay too. Coming from stock Android or another ROM, installing it wipes the phone, so back up first.

───────────────────────

✨ What's New & Improved
==============================

1. 🧠 On-device AI can connect again
- Before: a Google config file in ExpressiveOS let only the Now Playing app connect to Private Compute Services, the part of Android that downloads models for Google's on-device features. Android turned everything else away, so AICore, Gboard and Android System Intelligence couldn't even ask for their models or updates.
- Now: they can reach it again, as on a stock Pixel. Now Playing keeps recognizing songs as before.
- Thanks to Logan in the Expressive Group for the log that found it.

2. 🏠 Pixel Launcher, if you want it
- Before: Expressive Launcher was your only home screen.
- Now: Expressive Studio > Themes > Home screen > Launcher switches between Expressive Launcher and Pixel Launcher. The phone restarts once, and the launcher you picked runs both your home screen and recent apps. Switch back the same way. Expressive Launcher stays the default.
- If you use a navigation bar style, pick it again after switching launchers.

3. 🔍 Circle to Search
- Before: touching and holding the navigation handle did nothing. The Circle to Search setting was never switched on, and Expressive Launcher couldn't start it either.
- Now: hold the navigation handle to search what's on your screen, with Expressive Launcher or Pixel Launcher. It's on from the start, and you can turn it off in Settings > System > Circle to Search. Expressive Launcher 5.1.3, built in, adds the missing piece on its side.

💬 Community Notes
==============================

- Who it's for: the Pixel (cheetah) only. Install the build made for your exact phone.
- Updating from 1.1.1, 1.1 or 1.0: open the Updater and install 1.1.2; your apps and data stay. If you added root yourself, an update replaces the boot images, so add it again afterwards.
- Installing fresh: ExpressiveOS's packages don't carry the bootloader or modem. Flash Google's latest Android 17 factory image for your phone first, then ExpressiveOS, with the web installer or fastboot.
- Found a bug? Post it in the Bugs topic of the Expressive Group on Telegram.

🧩 Release Riddle
==============================

I'm one line in a config file, and I kept every app but one away from your phone's on-device AI. 1.1.2 deleted me. What am I?

Answer: An allow-association rule! With Now Playing as the only app on it, Android blocked AICore and Gboard. Now the door is open for them again. 🧠
